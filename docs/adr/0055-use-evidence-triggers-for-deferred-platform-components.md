# Use evidence triggers for deferred Platform components

OpenChoreo requires at least three Products or ten Cells plus measured repeated lifecycle work and a replaceable Provider PoC; OpenBao requires a proven managed-Secret compliance, private-deployment, or PKI gap; Harbor requires replication, offline, private-deployment, or advanced image-governance demand; and Crossplane requires demonstrated multi-cloud or high-volume self-service infrastructure provisioning. Their presence in the architectural baseline is not authority to deploy them without the stated evidence and an exit plan.
