# Use a Platform-owned notification gateway for outbound communication

ADR-0015 disabled Svix and the independent notification service because inbound payment webhooks belong to Platform Commerce, but nothing has since replaced outbound delivery. Stage 1 already requires outbound communication: registration and password-reset verification codes behind Casdoor, invoice issued/voided notices (ADR-0054), Emergency Access post-incident notice and review (ADR-0048), membership invitations (T05), and balance/billing alerts. This ADR fills that gap without reopening ADR-0015: inbound webhooks stay in Platform Commerce; outbound delivery becomes a Platform-owned capability.

The Platform runs a notification gateway behind a typed Provider/Adapter port, consistent with the platform principle that business facts and lifecycle stay Platform-owned while infrastructure is replaceable. Channel adapters cover SMS and email; final providers are gated by the external-confirmation register (mainland SMS real-name and template review, email deliverability) before production use. Delivery uses a transactional outbox in Platform PostgreSQL: business modules insert a notification record in the same transaction as the triggering fact, a dispatcher sends it through the adapter with idempotency per notification_id and bounded retry with backoff, and delivery state (sent/failed/expired) is tracked for audit. Verification codes are short-lived one-time values with per-recipient rate limiting and fraud throttling; codes never enter logs, traces, metrics, evidence, or the Audit stream. Message content is template-based and content-minimized: no secrets, no raw payment payloads, no prompts or document bodies, no more identity data than the template requires. Every notification emits a content-minimized Audit event (recipient hash, template type, timestamps, outcome) per ADR-0041, and provider credentials live in the managed Secret Manager per ADR-0026.

Consequences: the gateway is one more Day-1 component with two replaceable adapters, so it must not sit on the Lighthouse critical path until its dossier passes; verification-dependent flows (invitation via link, invoice notice) degrade to link/copy or in-app visibility when the channel is unavailable, and fail closed for security-critical notifications rather than silently dropping them. Accepted by wuyj (repository owner acting as adjudicator) on 2026-08-25; the ruling is recorded on issue #131, where provider-fact collection for the notification-channel dossier (T86.11) remains open work before production use of any channel.
