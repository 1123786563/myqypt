# Make P0 Production Gates non-waivable

Tenant isolation, cross-Tenant security, License, payment and Usage reconciliation, Usage replay, backup and restore, Tenant Erasure, Secret rotation, image signature, and Product Adapter compatibility cannot be waived for a paid launch. Platform engineering, security, finance or billing, and product jointly approve reproducible evidence, and no health check, focused test, or successful Workflow may be substituted for the required acceptance boundary.
